Over the last years, Ferrous Systems has not only qualified a compiler, but also helped multiple customers to get their robotics system certified for safety. This talk gives a light intro why certification of a system is meaningful and what it entails. It then deep-dives into how to actually execute on those things in Rust. Because Rust has much more to bring than just memory safety!
