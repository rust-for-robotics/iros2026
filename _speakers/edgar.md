Edgar Riba is the founder and project lead of Kornia AI, a non-profit organization stewarding a family of open-source computer vision and robotics projects — including Kornia (a differentiable CV library with 11K+ GitHub stars and over 2.9M monthly downloads), kornia-rs (Rust-native CV for the edge), Bubbaloop (a Rust platform for Physical AI), and work on SLAM and spatial perception. Edgar holds a PhD in Computer Science from Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona and an MSc in Automatic Control and Robotics from UPC. He has built autonomy pipelines at Bonsai Robotics, Farm-ng, Lightning AI, and Arraiy, served on the OpenCV Foundation steering committee, and mentors students through Google Summer of Code.
