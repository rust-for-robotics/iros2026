Tim Saucer is a Software Engineer at Rerun.io, where he works on data infrastructure and query systems for physical AI. His robotics career spans industrial automation at Dassault Systèmes (DELMIA), government-sponsored autonomy research at SoarTech, and autonomous vehicle infrastructure at May Mobility. He has worked on four DARPA robotics projects and led one of them. Tim holds a PhD in Physics from the University of Michigan. He is a PMC member and active contributor to Apache DataFusion, the open-source Rust query engine.
